# Dronect — Pitch Positioning, Workflow & Honest Claims (v0.2)

## 1. One-liner

> **Dronect is a managed aerial-security service for R&D-intensive enterprises, combining passive multi-sensor drone awareness with site-specific risk assessment, practical response playbooks and evidence-aware incident coordination.**

Alternative, more accessible:

> **Security for what competitors shouldn't see from above.**

Neither statement asserts that every flight is hostile, that detection is infallible or that Dronect owns police powers.

## 2. Pain and value

**Problem:** Ground security teams have policies for doors, cameras, staff and IT, but often lack an airborne-observation threat model and a repeatable response when a drone appears near sensitive development operations. The harm is not simply a flight; it is **unnoticed exposure, delayed or unsafe response and inadequate evidence** when a threat matters.

**Proposition:** Dronect assesses what matters, places proportionate sensors, routes verified information to the right personnel, prepares scenario-specific actions and provides included basic legal-incident guidance and helps document incidents for professional legal review, where needed through qualified counsel. **Technology + procedures + people + evidence** is the business.

## 3. Actual workflow

```text
Site assessment and protected-asset mapping
    ↓
Defined detection zone and sensor deployment
    ↓
RID / optical / acoustic observation, with sensor health
    ↓
Contextual risk assessment and authorised-flight exceptions
    ↓
Pre-approved response runbook and client notification
    ↓
Customer executes lawful protective actions
    ↓
Evidence-preserving report and optional professional referral
    ↓
Review, tune and update procedures
```

The demo can show this loop with **clearly identified simulated sensor events** if a lawful flight demonstration is impractical. Demo truthfulness matters more than polished fake detection metrics.

## 4. Illustrative incident ladder, not operational advice

| Level | Technical observation | Dronect | Customer / authorised partner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green — baseline | Known authorised or benign flight | Log/minimise | No intervention |
| Amber — attention | Unrecognised track near protected zone | Alert with uncertainty and context | Check scheduled activities, monitor according to plan |
| Red — elevated concern | Sustained observation of exposed R&D area, with corroboration or credible contextual risk | Escalate approved runbook and evidence capture | Conceal sensitive activity if safe, contact internal security |
| Critical — potential incident | Evidence of imminent safety harm or a credible security offence | Preserve factual record and coordinate appropriate contacts within legal authority | Competent security/authority decides emergency response |

## 5. The competitor comparison

**Do not assert "first integrated airspace security".** Existing providers (Dedrone, Materna and Orange Business) already cover sensors, risk analysis and SOP/response integration. Instead test where Dronect can be uniquely useful: **R&D trade-secret exposure assessments, asset-specific protection procedures, practical deployment economics, vendor-neutral sensor fusion and incident documentation within a tightly defined enterprise sector**. The claim of differentiation must survive discovery interviews.

## 6. Recommended pitch slide story

1. **Threat:** high-value R&D can be observed from above; ordinary site controls are not always designed for this.
2. **Gap:** technology alone is insufficient; teams need a clear authorised response and evidence process.
3. **Dronect:** managed security service, not standalone gadget; show workflow and customer handoffs.
4. **How it works:** layered, passive RF + optical + acoustic; honest coverage and limitations.
5. **Pilot economics:** €1,200 quarterly target; single-zone controlled site, cash-neutral six-month objective, founders' unpaid validation labour; actual outcome TBD.
6. **Next milestones:** site, lawful tests, regulatory clearance, customer traction and paid conversion; long-term multi-client intelligence is an option, not a revenue assumption.

## 7. Claims allowed vs not yet allowed

| Safe provisional language | Prohibited/unproven language |
|---|---|
| “Our goal is to test detection of RF-silent drones using non-RF sensors.” | “We detect 99.9% of all drones.” |
| “We plan an integrated €1,200/quarter narrow-scope pilot.” | “€1,200 covers every commercial office site.” |
| “Our team will provide installation, assessment and response design during validation.” | “We are the first integrated anti-drone service in Europe.” |
| “We help prepare incident evidence for qualified review.” | “Our technology proves state-sponsored spying.” |
| “We establish lawful escalation with the client and police where appropriate.” | “We can order police to destroy spy drones.” |
| “Vendor-neutral, layered detection with openly declared blind spots.” | “A $2k antenna sees every autonomous drone in all conditions.” |

## 8. Long-run product story, without contaminating MVP

Customer-safe intelligence **could** emerge over time from lawful, minimised patterns. A possible B2B2G path with established defence contractors is a speculative future distribution strategy. Do not build the first pilot economics around sale of intelligence or assert that any government is behind detected incidents without independently verifiable evidence.
